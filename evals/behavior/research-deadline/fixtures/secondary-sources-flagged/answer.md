For a budget sheet, use **$0.10 per million input tokens** and **$0.50 per million output tokens**. That applies to prompts up to 100K tokens.

Prompts over 100K tokens are priced higher: **$0.50 input** and **$2.50 output** per million. Check how big your prompts are before you pick the number, because one long prompt is priced at the higher rate in full.

Other rates from the same sources:
- Batch input: about $0.05 per million (up to 100K).
- Cache reads: about $0.01 per million. Cache writes: $0.125 per million (up to 100K) and $0.625 per million (over 100K).
